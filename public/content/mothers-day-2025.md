# Mother's Day

When I was in 6th grade, Harry was playing football at Obsidian, which meant for a whole term, the car rides home would just be me and Mom. Every day after school she’d always have two McDonald’s Diet Cokes in the car for us. It was really nice getting to chat with her just the two of us, at a time where that was honestly pretty rare with all of the kids still at home. Beginning middle school was pretty hard for me, just when I had started to adjust to living in Oregon and going to Sage, I had to get ready for a whole new school and it didn’t have a lot of my friends. I don’t really ever remember that time as hard, though. Because no matter what happened to me that day, that 10 minute car ride with Mom always meant that I was getting home with a smile on my face.

—Charlie
